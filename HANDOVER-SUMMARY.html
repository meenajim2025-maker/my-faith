My Faith — Handover Summary

Version 2.0 · Last updated 17 May 2026
Folder: C:\Users\reias\OneDrive\Desktop\My Faith\my-faith

What it is: A gentle story-led wellbeing app — pause, honesty, forgiveness, hope — for everyone including beginners. Story Paths, Life Mirror, First Path, reflection builder. Not emergency or clinical care.

2. Main product themes

  • Universal love — one love, many paths
  • God’s image in humanity — love waits for honest asking
  • Honour women, the poor, the overlooked — not the powerful
  • Hope for those who cannot ask (children, illness, oppression, grief)
  • Hope stronger than despair — real help includes safeguarding and care

3. Features built (May 2026 checkpoint)

  • Home — “Life gets noisy” opening + 7 feeling buttons → stories / Start Here / First Path
  • Story Paths — 1 live story (“The Message You Should Not Send”); more planned
  • Start Here — guided entry flow on home + tab
  • Life Mirror 2.0 — story before questions; tensions on steps
  • The First Path — 10 lessons, each opens with a scene
  • Reflection builder — choose need (apologise, forgive, courage, etc.) then words
  • Daily gentle step — rotating test + tension in banner
  • Universal hope · Path of Unparalleled Love · scenarios · meditation · journal · quiet mode · PWA
