# Phase 5 — The Content Canon

**The permanent heart of My Faith**  
(Vision + content rules only. This document is the blueprint for future writing and implementation — not executable code.)

---

## 1. What the Content Canon Is (and Why It Matters)

The Content Canon is the **unchanging spiritual constitution** of My Faith. It answers three questions forever:

1. What stories do we tell?
2. How do we speak?
3. What do we never become?

If Phase 4 gave **identity**, Phase 5 gives **depth, longevity, and trust**.

If everything else changes — technology, UI, platforms — the Canon must still feel **true, human, and faithful**.

---

## 2. The Golden Content Rules (Never Break These)

These rules apply to **every sentence** in the app.

### Always

- Speak to the **human heart**, not the intellect first
- Use **plain, gentle** language
- Centre **Jesus’ way of love**, not religious power
- **Invite reflection**, never force agreement
- **Honour silence** as much as words

### Never

- Argue doctrine
- Attack other beliefs
- Shame behaviour
- Use fear language
- Use insider church jargon **without explanation**
- Say **“you must”** or **“you should”**

### Canonical tone test

> Could a tired 16-year-old read this **without feeling judged**?  
> If not — **rewrite**.

---

## 3. The Three Sacred Content Pillars

Everything in My Faith belongs to **one** of these pillars.

### Pillar 1: Love of God

Trust · Meaning · Prayer · Returning when lost · Quiet faith

### Pillar 2: Love of Neighbour

Kindness · Forgiveness · Conscience · Justice without anger · Seeing the overlooked

### Pillar 3: Interior Freedom

Letting go of fear · Resisting comparison · Discernment · Healing shame · Integrity

These three subtly hold the spirit of the **Ten Commandments** without naming them, and fully live the **two great commandments** of Jesus.

---

## 4. The Jesus Corpus (Core Stories Only)

Approved **Jesus narrative set** — chosen to avoid controversy and maximise universality.

- No genealogy  
- No political conflict  
- No condemnation  

### Canonical Jesus themes

| Theme | Focus | Core insight |
|--------|--------|----------------|
| **1. Jesus Notices** | The unseen, excluded, tired, wounded | **Attention is the first act of love.** |
| **2. Jesus Heals Gently** | Without humiliation, perfectionism, or spectacle | **Healing often begins with being believed.** |
| **3. Jesus Forgives Without Weakness** | Forgiveness ≠ approval; mercy ≠ denial of truth | **Forgiveness frees the one who carries the wound.** |
| **4. Jesus Speaks Truth Calmly** | No shouting, domination, emotional violence | **Truth does not need cruelty to be strong.** |
| **5. Jesus Serves Quietly** | Washing feet, sharing meals, walking alongside | **Love becomes real when it kneels.** |

## 5. Mary Canon (Model, Not Debate)

Mary’s role must remain **human, relational, and dignified** — never controversial.

### Approved Mary themes

Trust in uncertainty · Deep listening · Strength without control · Quiet courage · Presence without dominance

### Mary framing rule

> **Mary is never explained — only encountered.**

**Example language:**  
*Mary teaches us how to stand still when life feels uncertain.*

**Avoid:** apparitions, arguments, hierarchy debates.

---

## 6. Apostolic Canon (Human Faith)

The apostles are presented as:

**ordinary · inconsistent · learning · growing**

### Approved messages

- Faith grows through relationship  
- Failure does not disqualify love  
- Community shapes belief  

### Avoid

- Authority hierarchies  
- Institutional power  
- Moral superiority  

---

## 7. The Life Scenarios Canon

All life scenarios must follow this structure:

1. **Name** the experience (without judgement)  
2. **Reveal** the inner tension  
3. **Offer** a Jesus-shaped insight  
4. **Suggest** one small act  
5. **Optional** prayer  

### Approved scenario categories

Anger · Fear · Guilt · Loneliness · Comparison · Burnout · Conflict · Forgiveness · Discernment · Self-worth

### Forbidden approaches

- “This is a sin”  
- “You must repent”  
- “Here’s the rule”  

**Always:** gentle honesty + practical love.

---

## 8. The Prayer Canon (Crucial)

Prayer is where My Faith becomes **sacred**.

### Prayer rules

- **Short** over long  
- **Honest** over polished  
- **Breathable** over dense  
- **Optional** over forced  

### Approved prayer styles

One sentence · One breath · One intention · One release  

**Example:**  
*God of love, calm my hurried heart.*

### Never

Threatening · Emotional manipulation · Overly poetic · Guilt-inducing  

---

## 9. Quiet Mode Canon (Global Gateway)

Quiet Mode is a **parallel canon**, not a reduced one.

### Quiet Mode rules

- No Catholic vocabulary  
- No Jesus titles  
- No scripture citations  
- No clerical language  

### But

Love remains · Wisdom remains · Meaning remains  

Quiet Mode should feel like **wisdom literature**, not religion. This protects **global reach**.

---

## 10. Language Blacklist (Permanent)

These words should **not** appear in **core user journeys**:

damnation · hell · devil / satan · heresy · moral failure · impurity · punishment · obedience language  

Not because they are “wrong” in all contexts — but because they **break psychological safety** for many modern users.

---

## 11. Content Review Test (Before Anything Publishes)

Every piece of content must pass **all five** tests:

| Test | Question |
|------|-----------|
| **Peace** | Does this calm **before** it instructs? |
| **Dignity** | Does this respect the reader’s **freedom**? |
| **Jesus** | Would this fit **how Jesus actually treated people**? |
| **Global** | Would this work in a **culture unlike my own**? |
| **Silence** | If read **slowly**, does it still feel **true**? |

**Fail one → revise.**

## 12. Universal Wisdom Canon (Gentle Thread)

This canon sits **beside** the **Unparalleled Love** corpus — never replacing it, never arguing with other faiths.

**Welcoming names (use in copy, not academic titles):**

| Instead of | Use |
|------------|-----|
| Jesus | **Unparalleled Love** (or **Love Himself** when needed) |
| Gospels | **the sacred stories** |
| Bhagavad Gita / Gita | **the Dialogue of Courage** |
| Upanishads | **the Wisdom of Stillness** |
| Mary | **a faithful mother** |
| Apostles | **companions on the path** |

### Always

- Centre **universal love** — one reality, many voices  
- Explain complex ideas in **plain, hopeful** language  
- Honour **women, the poor, the tired, and the overlooked** in stories and examples  
- Teach **honest asking** as the door to guidance and healing  
- Hold **hope** as stronger than bereavement and despair  

### Never

- Debate caste, rank, or inequality systems  
- Glorify the **rich, famous, or powerful** as models  
- Use the Gita to justify hatred, revenge, or cruelty  
- Blame those who **cannot ask** (children, severe illness, profound mental struggle, oppression)  
- Pretend prayer replaces **safeguarding, medical care, or justice** for the trapped  

### Core teachings (plain language)

| Theme | Heart of the teaching |
|--------|------------------------|
| **Image of love** | God entrusts humans with something of his own likeness — love waits for partners, not puppets. |
| **Ask** | Honest asking opens guidance; without asking, love does not force a closed heart. |
| **Act without hatred** | Do what protects the weak; do not let rage own the soul (Gita-shaped duty). |
| **Those who cannot ask** | Worth unchanged; love meets them through mercy, carers, and justice. |
| **Hope** | The last word — grief is honoured; despair is not shamed; one loving step still counts. |

**Quiet Mode:** same heart; soften religious names; keep universal love and hope.

### Wisdom themes (writers only — never name sources in UI)

Inspiration is drawn quietly from universal human themes: release and stillness; return, mercy, and renewal; awareness and compassion; non-harm and truthfulness; duty, respect, and harmony; conscience under pressure; dignity, justice, and service; humble protection of the vulnerable.

Registry: `src/language/wisdomThemes.js`. The First Path maps each moment to these themes.

## 13. The Unspoken Promise of My Faith

The Canon exists to keep this promise:

- If you come here **wounded**, you will not be wounded **again**.  
- If you come here **curious**, you will not be **trapped**.  
- If you come here **faithful**, you will be **deepened**, not inflamed.
