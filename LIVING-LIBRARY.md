# Phase 6 — The Living Library

**How My Faith grows without losing its soul**  
(Human blueprint only — not executable code. Use with **`CONTENT-CANON.md`** (Phase 5).)

This phase defines **what content exists**, **how it expands**, and **how it stays alive for decades** without drifting. It prevents **dilution**, **controversy**, and **burnout**.

---

## 1. What the Living Library Is

The Living Library is **not** a content dump.

It is a **curated, renewing** body of wisdom that:

- grows **slowly**
- repeats **safely**
- **deepens** rather than expands endlessly
- feels **timeless**, not seasonal
- **never chases trends**

The goal is not “new every day” — the goal is **“true every time.”**

---

## 2. The Three Content Layers (Very Important)

**Every piece of content belongs to one layer only.**

### Layer 1 — The Core (Unchanging)

**The permanent heart.**

- Jesus moments  
- Mary reflections  
- Fundamental life wisdom  
- Core prayers  

**Rules**

- Rarely changed  
- Carefully written  
- Reviewed deeply  
- **Never reactive** to news or culture  

Think: **scripture-level stability** (without calling it scripture).

---

### Layer 2 — The Breath (Slowly Renewing)

**What keeps the app feeling alive.**

- Daily reflections  
- “Try today” prompts  
- Gentle reminders  
- Weekly challenges  

**Rules**

- Written in **cycles**  
- **Reusable yearly**  
- Calm and grounded  
- **Never urgent**  

Think: **seasons of the soul**, not the calendar.

---

### Layer 3 — The Response (Contextual, Limited)

**Optional and carefully controlled.**

- Reflections for grief  
- Times of stress  
- Global uncertainty  
- Personal struggle  

**Rules**

- **Rare**  
- Carefully worded  
- **No commentary**  
- **No blame**  
- **No politics**  

Think: **a calm voice in the storm** — not analysis of the storm.

---

## 3. The Canonical Jesus Moments (Exact Set)

These are the **anchor moments** of the entire app.

**There should be 12 core Jesus moments — no more, no less.**

| # | Moment |
|---|--------|
| 1 | Jesus notices the unnoticed |
| 2 | Jesus listens before speaking |
| 3 | Jesus shows mercy without weakness |
| 4 | Jesus forgives without denying truth |
| 5 | Jesus welcomes the excluded |
| 6 | Jesus heals without spectacle |
| 7 | Jesus challenges without humiliation |
| 8 | Jesus withdraws to pray |
| 9 | Jesus serves quietly |
| 10 | Jesus faces suffering without hatred |
| 11 | Jesus trusts God in uncertainty |
| 12 | Jesus gives himself in love |

**Each moment has:**

- one **story** (plain language)  
- one **insight**  
- one **human question**  
- one **small act**  

These **never expire**.

*(Implementation note for later: current app has 7 bundled moments — expand/align to this set of 12 when ready.)*

---

## 4. The Daily Reflection Engine (Timeless Design)

### Key principle

Daily reflections must **not depend on dates**.

**No:**

- “Monday motivation”  
- “Sunday reflection”  
- Church calendar dependency  
- News references  

**Instead:**  
Reflections rotate by **human rhythm**, not time.

### Reflection categories

Love · Trust · Forgiveness · Courage · Patience · Peace · Integrity · Gratitude · Discernment · Compassion  

### Each reflection has

- **60–90 words max**  
- **one action**  
- **one optional prayer**  

The library can be as small as **60 reflections**, reused endlessly.

---

## 5. Age-Aware, Not Age-Separated

My Faith **never splits** the library by age.

Instead, each reflection has an **age-tone variation**.  
Same truth, different voice:

- Teenager  
- Adult  
- Older person  
- Explorer  

This preserves **unity** while respecting **development**.

---

## 6. The Life Mirror Library (Expansion Rules)

The Life Mirror grows by **human experience**, not theology.

### Allowed expansion themes

Conflict · Shame · Comparison · Loneliness · Fear · Exhaustion · Hope · Decision-making · Forgiveness · Self-worth  

### Forbidden expansion triggers

**Never** create content simply because:

- it’s trending  
- it’s controversial  
- others demand a position  
- social media is loud  

**Silence is a valid editorial choice.**

---

## 7. Prayer Library Rules (Permanent)

The app should **never exceed** these prayer limits:

| Type | Max words |
|------|-----------|
| Short | ≤ 40 |
| Medium | ≤ 80 |
| Long | ≤ 120 *(rare)* |

### Prayer types allowed

asking · thanking · releasing · trusting · forgiving  

### Banned in prayer

- threats  
- promises of reward or punishment  
- emotional pressure  
- “God will only…” language  

---

## 8. The Silence Principle (Unique Globally)

**Silence is content.**

The app should regularly:

- **pause**  
- leave **space**  
- **not** explain everything  
- allow **emptiness**  

You are building something closer to a **chapel** than a **feed**.

---

## 9. The AI Boundary (Essential for Trust)

### If AI is ever used — AI **may**

- help draft language  
- paraphrase gently  
- simplify sentences  
- generate variations **under strict rules**  

### AI **may never**

- invent theology  
- respond emotionally  
- personalise moral judgement  
- simulate authority  
- speak “as God”  

**All AI output must feel human-reviewed.**

---

## 10. The Editorial Council Model (Even if It’s One Person)

Even if you are the only editor, **every content piece** is checked as if approved by:

- A teacher  
- A pastoral carer  
- A young person  
- A sceptic  
- A hurt believer  

**If one would be harmed — revise.**

---

## 11. The Growth Safeguard (Protects the Project)

As My Faith grows:

- Content grows **slower**, not faster  
- New sections require **removal or refinement** elsewhere  
- **Strong** content replaces **weak** content  
- **Silence increases** as library matures  

**Growth = depth, not volume.**

---

## 12. The Unchanging North Star

This sentence governs everything:

> **If this app disappeared tomorrow, would what remains in a person be love, peace, and clarity?**  
> If **yes** — the Living Library is healthy.
