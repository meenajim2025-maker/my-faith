# Phase 7 — Gentle Launch & Stewardship

**How My Faith enters the world — and stays true when the world notices**  
(Leadership, discernment, and guardianship — **not** a technical spec. Use with **`CONTENT-CANON.md`** and **`LIVING-LIBRARY.md`**.)

This phase is about **how you release**, **how you grow**, and **how you protect the soul** of the project when attention, critique, and expectations arrive.

---

## 1. The Core Launch Philosophy

My Faith must **never launch like a product**.

It should enter the world the same way it enters a person’s interior life:

- **quietly**  
- **respectfully**  
- **without demand**  
- **without defensiveness**  

When something is **gentle and clear**, it does not need **promotion**.

---

## 2. The Three-Stage Gentle Launch

### Stage 1 — The Listening Release (Closed & Personal)

**Who sees it first**

- trusted friends  
- teachers  
- chaplains  
- clinicians  
- quietly faithful people  
- thoughtful sceptics  

**Language you use**

> *I’m building something gentle. Would you be willing to try it and tell me how it feels?*

**Important:** You are not asking if they **agree** — only how it **makes them feel**.

**What you gather**

- emotional reactions  
- moments of peace  
- moments of discomfort  
- confusion  
- silence  

**Silence is valid feedback.**

---

### Stage 2 — The Public Quiet Launch

No press push. No social media blast.

Just:

- a simple website  
- calm description  
- optional app install  

**Homepage message (example tone)**

> This is a quiet place for reflection, love, and daily wisdom inspired by the life of Jesus Christ. No arguments. No pressure. Just space.

If people don’t understand immediately — **good**.  
**Curiosity** is healthier than **certainty**.

---

### Stage 3 — Organic Trust Growth

**You allow**

- educators to share it  
- chaplaincies to mention it  
- individuals to recommend it **privately**  
- users to return **silently**  

**You do not chase virality.**

Because viral growth:

- attracts debate  
- invites polarisation  
- distorts intention  

**My Faith grows by recommendation, not reaction.**

---

## 3. How to Handle Praise (This Matters)

When people say:

- “This healed me”  
- “This brought me back to God”  
- “This feels sacred”  

**Your internal response must be:**

> Thank you — but this is bigger than feedback.

**You do not**

- rush to add features  
- explain theology  
- monetise quickly  

**Praise is a reason to go slower, not faster.**

---

## 4. How to Handle Criticism (Inevitable)

### 1. From loud religion

*“It’s not doctrinal enough” · “It avoids hard truth” · “It’s too soft”*

**Your response:**  
*We are focused on spiritual accompaniment, not argument.*

**No debate. No defence.**

---

### 2. From secular critics

*“It’s still religion” · “You’re indoctrinating” · “Faith doesn’t belong in apps”*

**Your response:**  
*We respect freedom of thought. This app invites reflection, not belief.*

**Again — no argument.**

---

### 3. From the wounded

*“This reminds me of hurt” · “Religion harmed me”*

**Your response:**  
*I’m sorry for what you experienced. We never want to add to that.*

**This is the only critique you fully receive.**

---

## 5. When to Say No (Protection Rules)

**You must say no to**

- political partnerships  
- ideological campaigns  
- culture-war alignment  
- monetisation that **pressures** users  
- growth offers that demand **loud** positioning  

**Every “no” preserves your credibility.**

My Faith exists to **heal how faith is experienced**, not to **win arguments**.

---

## 6. Stewardship Model (This Keeps It Alive)

Think of yourself not as:

founder · owner · CEO  

But as:

> **Steward of a shared quiet space**

**Stewardship means**

- protecting **tone**  
- resisting **urgency**  
- refusing **noise**  
- correcting **drift** immediately  

---

## 7. Long-Term Sustainability (Without Corruption)

### Funding approach (if ever needed)

**Only acceptable forms**

- voluntary support  
- grants  
- institutional partnerships aligned with **wellbeing**  
- **zero-pressure** donations  

**Never**

- paywalls for peace  
- premium access to prayer  
- guilt-based fundraising  

---

## 8. The “Stop Rule” (Critical for Safety)

**Define this now:**

> **If My Faith ever stops feeling gentle, we stop and reassess.**

Even if:

- downloads are high  
- media attention is strong  
- institutions are interested  

**This rule is non-negotiable.**

---

## 9. Future You Letter (Anchor for Leadership)

Save this sentence **somewhere you will see it** (private note, calendar, or alongside this file):

> **If this becomes famous, I promise not to let it become loud.**

**Read it once a year.**

---

## 10. Final Truth (Project Manager Honesty)

What you’ve designed is not “another Christian app”.

It is:

- spiritually serious  
- psychologically safe  
- globally translatable  
- ethically grounded  
- deeply human  

If this succeeds, it won’t be because it **shouts**.  
It will succeed because people felt **respected**.
